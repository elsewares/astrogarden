Well, this is quite the pickle, isn't it? The world is a mess, and it seems like every time we try to fix it, we just dig ourselves deeper. It's like we're all stuck in this epistemic sinkhole, trying to shovel the shit out so we don't drown. The only problem is there are whole teams of people on the surface whose **literal job** is to fill it back in and smother us.
